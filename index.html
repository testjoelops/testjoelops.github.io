Staardust

In the Star Wars universe, Stardust was the codename for the secret Death Star project, the Galactic Empire's planet-destroying superweapon. The name was chosen by Galen Erso, the brilliant scientist forced to oversee the Death Star's development, as a tribute to his daughter, Jyn Erso, whom he affectionately called "Stardust." He secretly embedded a critical flaw into the Death Star's design and ensured that the stolen Stardust plans would lead Jyn and the Rebel Alliance to discover this vulnerability.
