Staardust

The Empire's ultimate weapon. Conceived in secrecy, engineered across decades, and powered by unimaginable technology, Project Stardust gave rise to the Death Star, a battle station built to impose fear on an entire galaxy. Forged through unparalleled ambition and ruthless precision, it stood as the pinnacle of Imperial engineering, where every component, every calculation, and every decision served a single purpose: absolute dominance.

Yet beneath its flawless design lay a single weakness, proving that even the greatest creations can be undone by hope. In the end, Project Stardust became more than a weapon, it became a reminder that no empire is invincible, and that the smallest spark of rebellion can change the fate of the galaxy forever.
